Admissions

Joining St. Martin's Mwibale.

We admit at Form 1 through KPSEA placement, and accept transfers into Form 2–3 on a case-by-case basis.

  1. Collect an application form

    Pick one up at the school office, or request a copy on WhatsApp — details in the footer below.

  2. Submit KCPE / KPSEA results slip

    Bring the original and one photocopy, plus a copy of the learner's birth certificate.

  3. Meet the admissions committee

    A short interview with the learner and at least one parent or guardian, held on campus.

  4. Confirm the slot

    Pay the admission deposit to secure placement ahead of the term opening date.
